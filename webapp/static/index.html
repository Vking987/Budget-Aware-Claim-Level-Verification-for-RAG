    What is real in this demo, and what is not
    • Real: retrieval (live Wikipedia or local Qdrant), the draft (Llama-3.2-1B), the rule-based decomposition and CTS, the policy's scores, the verifier's verdicts, the 1B repairer, re-verification and the controller's budget accounting. Events are streamed as they happen.
    • LinUCB is frozen. It was trained offline and does not learn during a run. The reward shown is computed from the verifier's verdict and is not used to update anything. CTS ranking is contextual scoring without learning; LinUCB is the contextual bandit.
    • Verdicts are not ground truth. In our checks the 1B assessor was near chance and the NLI verifier caught about half of known-hallucinated claims. “Insufficient” means the retrieved passages neither support nor contradict the claim.
    • Not implemented here: Thompson Sampling, oracle or simulated feedback (offline experiments only), the D2R-RAG baseline, and any factuality or support metric that needs human annotation.
